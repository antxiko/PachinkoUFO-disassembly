A pachinko machine, then another and another: the one whose centre opens, the one with the slot machine and the one with the UFOs. The strength of each ball, the pins in a bitmap whose half is a mirror, the pockets, the V, the openings and the big UFO; the title from the ROM's bytes, checked against openMSX; the three machines in GIF and the four tunes from its own engine, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1984 - 16 KB cartridge at 0x8000 - MSX - Dump cad65376...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The logo and the title come from running the ROM (checked against openMSX), and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the controls, the balls, the objective, the levels and the endings, as the code has them.
  • The machines: the three machines, played in GIF: the centre that opens, the slot machine and the UFOs.
  • The sprites: the launcher, the windmills, the ball and the U.F.O.s in their colours, put together from the ROM tables.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the four tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: a machine drawn in half, a sound hidden among the tiles, ten that are sixteen and the code nobody calls.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the BIOS it uses, the interrupt, the texts behind the calls, the RAM and the tools.
